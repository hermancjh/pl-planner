PL Plan BuilderSchool Professional Learning Plan

    Work flows top-down: each layer feeds the next. Your Total Learning Plan is built from the initiatives you define under Objectives.

    Build your school's Professional Learning Plan

    Fill each layer of the planning cascade. Everything saves automatically as you go. When you're done, export the data to keep working later, or save the whole plan as a PDF.

    Layer 1

    Vision, Mission & Values

    State the school's guiding intent. Add further statements (e.g. a learning philosophy or motto) if your school features more at this level.

    Layer 2

    Operating Context & Creative Tensions

    Name the gaps between aspiration and current reality. Use the structured stem for each tension. Add as many as your context demands.

    Layer 3

    Strategic Thrusts

    The school's directions in response to the tensions. At least one thrust should address staff development — that is the one your PL objectives will hang from.

    Layer 4

    PL Strategic Objectives & Initiatives

    Translate the staff-development thrust into objectives, then branch each into initiatives anchored on a TGM Learning Area or an SFEd Area of Practice.

    Layer 5

    Whole-School LEARN Evaluation

    For each LEARN dimension, note the school-level indicators you will track. E and N are enablers; R, L and A are outcomes. Programme-level indicators sit later, inside the Total Learning Plan.

    Layer 6

    Total Learning Plan

    Each initiative from Layer 4 appears below. For every learning experience, tag the A-R-C Learning Model and the Design Principles for Effective PL, then add the staff segment, resources and the programme-level LEARN indicators for that experience.
